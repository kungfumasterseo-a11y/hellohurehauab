Professional Building Cleaning Services in Senden, Ulm and Neu-Ulm

Keeping commercial and residential properties clean is not just about appearances, it is about creating an environment where employees, clients, and residents feel comfortable and confident.

RS Gebäudereinigung has spent over ten years building a reputation as a trusted cleaning partner for businesses, property managers, medical practices, and private clients across Senden, Ulm, and Neu-Ulm. With more than 250 properties under regular care, the company has grown steadily by focusing on consistency, reliability, and honest pricing rather than cutting corners.

One of the biggest frustrations property owners face with cleaning companies is constant staff turnover. A different person shows up every week, standards slip, and communication becomes a hassle. RS Gebäudereinigung takes a different approach by employing a fixed, permanently staffed team rather than relying on temporary workers. This means the same trained professionals return to a property again and again, learning its layout, its specific needs, and the preferences of the people who work or live there. Every property is also assigned a dedicated site supervisor who understands the client's requirements, trains the team accordingly, and carries out regular on-site quality checks. This structure keeps results consistent month after month, not just during the first few visits.

The range of services covers nearly everything a building might need. Regular maintenance cleaning is available for offices, medical practices, and commercial spaces, with schedules built around each client's actual opening hours, whether that means daily, weekly, or on-demand visits. Office cleaning covers desks, floors, kitchens, and restrooms, typically performed outside business hours so operations are never disrupted. Window and glass cleaning handles everything from storefronts to hard-to-reach façades, leaving streak-free results using professional equipment.

For situations requiring deeper attention, such as after a renovation, a move, or as an annual intensive clean, the deep cleaning service removes stubborn dirt and buildup that regular cleaning cannot reach. Construction cleaning is also available, covering everything from rough post-construction cleanup to the final detailed clean needed before a property can be handed over on schedule, which is especially valuable for developers and property managers working against deadlines. Stairwell and common area cleaning rounds out the offering, keeping entrances, staircases, and shared spaces presentable for property management companies and owner associations.

Pricing is handled transparently. After a free, no-obligation site visit, clients receive a clearly broken-down fixed price quote with no hidden fees or surprise charges later on. This upfront approach removes the guesswork that often comes with hiring a cleaning service and lets clients budget with confidence. All staff are properly employed, insured, and trained, and the company carries liability insurance to cover any potential damages, giving clients contractual peace of mind rather than vague assurances.

Sustainability is also part of the company's approach. RS Gebäudereinigung uses precisely dosed, biodegradable cleaning products along with water-saving methods, aiming to keep spaces clean without placing unnecessary strain on the environment.

The process for getting started is simple. Clients reach out by phone or message and typically receive a response within 24 hours. A free site visit follows to assess the actual scope of work, after which a written fixed price offer with a detailed service list is provided. Once approved, the assigned team begins on the agreed date, with the site supervisor continuing to monitor quality on an ongoing basis.

Client feedback reflects this consistency.
